[h1]霜誓者 · Frostsworn[/h1]
[b]冰封敌人的行动，把下一回合的胜机藏进寒冬。[/b]

霜誓者是一名加入《杀戮尖塔2》的冰霜法师。你可以用寒霜控制敌人、召唤冰晶发动攻击，也可以将卡牌冷藏，等待解冻后组织爆发。冰甲与雪势支撑攻防，而自霜让强大的力量伴随着需要承担的代价。

[h2]角色特色[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]寒霜与冰封[/b]：累积寒霜，达到敌人的冰封门槛后使其跳过一次行动。同一敌人被冰封得越多，后续门槛越高——把控制留给最关键的一回合。
[*][b]冷藏与解冻[/b]：将卡牌暂时封存，回合开始时解冻。解冻牌下次打出费用降低1点；通过抽牌、额外解冻和相关能力，为下一回合铺好连招。
[*][b]冰晶[/b]：召唤可打出的攻击牌，用强化、升级和联动让一枚冰晶汇成一场冰晶风暴。
[*][b]冰甲[/b]：在格挡之后抵挡伤害，使用时消耗，每回合自然融化。积攒防护，也要把握出手的时机。
[*][b]雪势[/b]：回合结束时对所有敌人造成非攻击伤害，随后层数减半。让不断积累的风雪成为持续的进攻。
[*][b]自霜[/b]：下回合失去生命，换取当下的收益。借助相关卡牌与能力，将风险纳入你的战斗计划。
[/list]

[h2]日食挑战[/h2]
除了霜誓者，本模组还为[b]所有角色[/b]提供日食0–8挑战。各级效果逐步叠加，改变恢复、资源与战斗条件。
[list]
[*][b]单人[/b]：每个角色分别记录最高日食难度。
[*][b]多人[/b]：由房主选择，全队使用同一难度；多人进度与单人分开，角色之间共享多人日食进度。
[/list]

[b]各级新增效果[/b]（高等级包含之前所有等级的效果）：
[list]
[*][b]日食0[/b]：无特殊效果。
[*][b]日食1[/b]：先古之民只会回复你已损失生命值的70%。
[*][b]日食2[/b]：隐藏每一幕首个Boss前的3层地图节点，无法看到房间类型。
[*][b]日食3[/b]：初始金币减少20。
[*][b]日食4[/b]：偶数回合开始时，敌人获得3点格挡。
[*][b]日食5[/b]：除先古之民外，所有来源的回复量减少三分之一；正数回复最低为1点。
[*][b]日食6[/b]：事件造成的生命损失增加50%；最大生命值的增减不受此项影响。
[*][b]日食7[/b]：奇数回合开始时，敌人获得1点临时力量，不被人工制品抵消。
[*][b]日食8[/b]：友方每次受到未被格挡的伤害且存活时，最大生命值减少1点，累计最多减少50%；下一幕开始时恢复由日食扣除的最大生命值。
[/list]

[h1]Frostsworn — Slay the Spire 2 Character Mod[/h1]
[b]Freeze your enemies. Store your next turn's winning play in the cold.[/b]

Frostsworn is an ice mage built around control, preparation, and bursts of frost magic. Freeze enemies with Frost, conjure Ice Crystals, or put cards into Cold Storage for a stronger turn ahead. Ice Armor and Snowfall support your defense and offense, while Self-Frost makes powerful plays come with a cost.

[h2]Playstyle[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Frost & Freeze[/b] — Reach an enemy's Freeze threshold to make it skip an action. Repeated Freezes raise that enemy's threshold, so time your control carefully.
[*][b]Cold Storage & Thaw[/b] — Store cards for later and Thaw them at the start of your turn. A Thawed card costs 1 less the next time it is played. Combine draw, extra Thaws, and supporting Powers to prepare your next combo.
[*][b]Ice Crystals[/b] — Create Attack cards, then strengthen, upgrade, and combine them through dedicated synergies.
[*][b]Ice Armor[/b] — Absorbs damage after Block and is consumed as it protects you. It naturally melts each turn, rewarding well-timed defense.
[*][b]Snowfall[/b] — Deals non-Attack damage to ALL enemies at the end of your turn, then halves its stacks.
[*][b]Self-Frost[/b] — Trade HP loss next turn for benefits now, and use supporting cards and Powers to manage the cost.
[/list]

[h2]Eclipse Challenge[/h2]
Adds [b]Eclipse 0–8 for ALL characters[/b], with cumulative modifiers to healing, resources, and combat.
Single-player progression is tracked separately for each character. In multiplayer, the host selects one difficulty for the whole party; multiplayer progression is separate from single-player and shared across characters.

[b]Modifiers added at each level[/b] (higher levels include all earlier modifiers):
[list]
[*][b]Eclipse 0[/b] — No special effects.
[*][b]Eclipse 1[/b] — Ancients restore only 70% of your missing HP.
[*][b]Eclipse 2[/b] — Hide the map nodes on the three floors before the first Boss of each Act, concealing their room types.
[*][b]Eclipse 3[/b] — Start with 20 less Gold.
[*][b]Eclipse 4[/b] — At the start of even-numbered turns, enemies gain 3 Block.
[*][b]Eclipse 5[/b] — Healing from all sources other than Ancients is reduced by one third. Positive healing restores at least 1 HP.
[*][b]Eclipse 6[/b] — HP loss from events is increased by 50%. Changes to Max HP are unaffected by this modifier.
[*][b]Eclipse 7[/b] — At the start of odd-numbered turns, enemies gain 1 temporary Strength. Cannot be negated by Artifact.
[*][b]Eclipse 8[/b] — Each time an ally takes unblocked damage and survives, they lose 1 Max HP, up to a total reduction of 50%. Max HP lost to Eclipse is restored at the start of the next Act.
[/list]

[h2]Settings & Error Reports[/h2]
In RitsuLib's mod settings, open [b]Frostsworn → Error reports[/b] to change [b]Allow sending error reports[/b].
This option is [b]on by default[/b]. Sanitized diagnostic logs from errors and multiplayer state divergence are sent to the author's [b]public GitHub repository[/b] for troubleshooting. No player login is required, and full saves are not sent. Turning it off stops collection and sending; updates preserve your choice.
